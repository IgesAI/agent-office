    Connect Grok Bot

    Bots created in Grok Bot can appear here with their exact names. Creating, renaming, and deleting Bots stays in Grok Bot.

    Experimental connection

    The optional local bridge uses an unofficial, pinned SDK. It reads your Bot roster after a separate browser sign-in. It does not send messages or run tasks.

    With --activity, the bridge also watches each Bot’s transcript (read-only) so Bots show what they were asked to do, walk to the matching room, and rest when quiet. Without it, Bots wander and show Activity unavailable.

    Local setup

    From the project directory, follow docs/grok-bot.md to prepare the reviewed SDK, then run:

    node scripts/grok-bot-bridge.mjs --experimental --login --activity

    Keep the bridge running while you use the house. No Grok Bot login is read from your computer.
